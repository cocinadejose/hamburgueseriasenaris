Santiago Airport: Updated Flights & Connections (2026-2027)

Updated: November 13, 2026 | By Susana

✅ Santiago-Rosalía de Castro Airport (SCQ / Lavacolla) is operating normally. Following a temporary runway closure from 23 April to 27 May 2026, the terminal is open and connects Santiago with Spain, Europe and the United States. This guide summarises flights for the winter 2026-2027 season, how to get from the airport to the city centre, and alternatives if you prefer to fly to A Coruña, Vigo, Porto or Madrid. Data has been cross-checked with Aena and Wikipedia, but we recommend confirming schedules and fares directly with the airlines before travelling.

At Señarís we help many pilgrims and travellers arriving in Santiago. We know how important it is to have clear, up-to-date information. We keep this guide current so you can plan your trip without surprises. Safe travels, and if you're walking the Camino — Buen Camino!

🚌 How to get from Santiago Airport (Lavacolla) to the city centre

The airport is about 12 km from downtown Santiago. You have three main options:

Tralusa/Tussa city bus — Line 6A

The cheapest option. It links the airport with Santiago city centre (Plaza de Galicia, bus station and other central stops).

  • Operator: Tralusa / Tussa.
  • Phone: +34 901 120 054 / +34 981 581 815.
  • Approximate fare: ~€1.50 (check current fare on the website).
  • Frequency: varies by day and time; check the official timetable.

Taxi

There is a taxi rank at the terminal. The trip to the centre takes about 15-20 minutes. The fare is usually around €20-25, but confirm the price with the driver before getting in.

Car hire

Several hire companies operate at the airport. From the terminal you join the A-54 directly, reaching central Santiago in about 15 minutes.

✈️ Airports to reach Santiago

Here in the neighbourhood we know how important it is to have all the info to plan your trip. That's why we've put together this guide with the main airports for reaching Santiago, including practical alternatives. Each has its own advantages, and we'll walk you through them in detail.

🟢 A Coruña Airport (Alvedro, LCG)

Distance to Santiago: about 65 km (~45 min by car).

How to get to Santiago: MonBus from A Coruña bus station to Santiago Intermodal Station. Indicative price: €8-10. Time: ~1h 15 min. From A Coruña Airport to the city centre you can take urban bus line A4 (Alsa).

Main routes: Madrid, Barcelona, Bilbao, Seville, Málaga, Palma de Mallorca, Gran Canaria, Tenerife, Valencia (some seasonal), and international flights to London-Gatwick, Geneva and Milan-Malpensa.

Ideal for: Domestic flights and some European connections when you can't find a direct ticket to Santiago.

🟡 Vigo Airport (Peinador, VGO)

Distance to Santiago: about 90 km (~1h 15 min by car).

How to get to Santiago: MonBus or Renfe train from Urzáiz Intermodal Station. The bus takes ~1h 15 min and the train ~50 min. From Vigo Airport to the centre take Vitrasa line A.

Main routes: Madrid, Barcelona, Gran Canaria, Tenerife North and Palma de Mallorca (seasonal). In past seasons there have also been flights to London-Stansted and Paris.

Ideal for: Domestic routes and if you find a better price from southern Galicia.

🔵 Porto Airport (Sá Carneiro, OPO)

Distance to Santiago: about 230 km (~2h 30 min by car).

How to get to Santiago: Several daily buses with ALSA, FlixBus and BlaBlaCar Bus from the airport or Campanhã station. Journey time: 3h 30 min to 5h. Prices from ~€9.99. You can also take the train from Porto-Campanhã to Vigo and continue to Santiago.

Main routes: International connections throughout Europe, Lisbon, Madrid, Barcelona, Paris, London, Frankfurt, Geneva, etc.

Ideal for: International travellers. It usually has more choice and better prices from outside Spain.

💡 If you're walking the Camino Portugués, see our Portuguese Way Guide from Porto.

🟠 Madrid-Barajas (MAD) + AVE

How to get to Santiago: AVE/Avlo high-speed train by Renfe from Madrid-Chamartín to Santiago de Compostela. Time: ~2h 45 min. Prices from ~€25 if booked in advance.

Main routes: Connections worldwide.

Ideal for: Those flying from America, Asia or any long-haul destination and preferring a final leg by train.

✈️ Direct flights from the UK and US to nearby airports

If you're flying from the UK, Ireland or the US, these are the most practical routes to get close to Santiago.

🇬🇧 From the UK to A Coruña (LCG)

London → A Coruña

Direct flights with Vueling (London-Gatwick and, in some seasons, London-Heathrow). Flight time: ~2h 15-30 min.

🇬🇧 From the UK to Porto (OPO)

London, Manchester, Edinburgh, Bristol, Liverpool → Porto

Direct flights with Ryanair, easyJet and Jet2. Flight time: ~2h 15-50 min. From Porto you can take a bus to Santiago (3h 30 min - 5h).

🇺🇸 From the US

New York (Newark) → Santiago (SCQ)

Direct flights with United Airlines, 3 times a week (Monday, Thursday, Saturday).

Other US cities

Fly to Madrid-Barajas and take the AVE train to Santiago, or fly to Porto with connections and continue by bus.

🥾 Why A Coruña is a special gateway: The Camino Inglés (English Way)

🇬🇧 The Camino Inglés — A historic route for British pilgrims

When you fly to A Coruña, you're following the historic Camino Inglés (English Way), a 75 km route (3-5 days) that links A Coruña or Ferrol with Santiago de Compostela.

It's a great option for a shorter pilgrimage or a long weekend.

🚌 How to get from the airports to Santiago

We know it's best to have everything well planned. Here are the connections step by step, so you don't have to worry about a thing.

🚍 A Coruña Airport (LCG) → A Coruña Bus Station

Line A4 (Alsa) / 4051

  • Frequency: Every 30 minutes on weekdays (7:15-21:45) and every 60 minutes on weekends and holidays.
  • Price: €1.55 single ticket.
  • Airport stop: Floor 0, arrivals area.
  • Connection: Directly connects with A Coruña bus station, among other stops in the city centre.

🚍 Vigo Bus Station → Vigo Airport (VGO)

Line A (Vitrasa)

  • Frequency: Approximately every 36 minutes on weekdays and Saturday afternoons, every 60 minutes on Sunday mornings.
  • Price: €2.00.
  • Stop at Vigo Bus Station: Line A stops at the bus station itself (within Urzáiz Intermodal Station).
  • Airport stop: Floor 0, opposite the arrivals terminal.

🚍 Porto Airport (OPO) → Santiago de Compostela

Up to 22 daily services

ALSA: 22 daily services, departures from 04:00 to 23:59. Journey time: 3h 59 min to 5h 00 min. Some departures from the airport at 04:30, 06:30, 06:45 and 07:00 arrive in Santiago at 08:30, 11:15 and 11:30. Arrives at Santiago Intermodal Station.

FlixBus: Up to 13 daily trips, first bus at 2:40 am, last at 5:10 pm. Journey time: ~3h 30 min. Price: between €9.99 and €19.24.

BlaBlaCar Bus: 2 times a day, journey time ~3h 25 min. Prices from €15. Departures at 14:45 and 16:55.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is Santiago de Compostela Airport open?

Yes. Santiago-Rosalía de Castro Airport (SCQ / Lavacolla) is open and operating normally after reopening on 27 May 2026. The temporary runway closure has ended.

How do I get from Santiago Airport to the city centre?

You have three main options: the Tralusa/Tussa city bus line 6A (cheapest, ~€1.50), a taxi (~€20-25 to the centre) or a hire car via the A-54.

Which airlines fly to Santiago de Compostela?

Airlines such as Ryanair, Vueling, Iberia, Air Nostrum, Air Europa, Wizz Air, easyJet, Norwegian, Lufthansa, KLM, United Airlines, Aer Lingus, Edelweiss Air, Swiss and others operate there. Schedules vary by season.

What are the best alternative airports?

The most practical are A Coruña (LCG) and Vigo (VGO), both less than 1h 30 min from Santiago. For international long-haul flights, Porto (OPO) is a very popular alternative with direct buses to Santiago.

Is there a direct flight from the United States?

Yes. United Airlines operates the Santiago – New York/Newark route with 3 weekly frequencies (Monday, Thursday and Saturday).

Is it worth flying to Madrid and then taking the AVE?

If you find a good flight to Madrid-Barajas, the AVE/Avlo from Chamartín to Santiago takes about 2h 45 min and can be a comfortable option. Book in advance for better prices.

📝 Final recommendations

  • Book buses, trains and flights in advance, especially during peak season and bank holidays.
  • If you fly to Santiago, remember the airport is 12 km from the centre: line 6A is the cheapest, taxis are quick, and hire cars are useful if you're touring Galicia.
  • If you can't find a direct flight, compare A Coruña, Vigo and Porto. The transfer time often compensates for a lower fare.
  • If you're coming from far away, Madrid-Barajas + AVE or Porto + bus are usually the most flexible combinations.
  • If you're planning to walk the Camino de Santiago, see our Portuguese Way Guide from Porto if you land in Portugal.
  • Always check official websites (Aena, airlines, Renfe, MonBus, ALSA, FlixBus). We're a burger bar and compile this information in good faith, but routes and schedules change.
