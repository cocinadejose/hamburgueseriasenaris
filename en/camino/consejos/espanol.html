🗣️ Basic Spanish for Pilgrims

Learning a few words of Spanish can make your Camino de Santiago experience much richer and easier. Most locals and many pilgrims speak little or no English, so knowing some basic Spanish will help you with accommodation, food, emergencies, and making friends along the way. Don’t be afraid to try—locals appreciate the effort!

  • Practice these phrases before your trip or on the road.
  • Use translation apps like Google Translate or SpanishDict for extra help.
  • Remember: a smile and a simple “¡Hola!” go a long way.

Cultural Tips

  • When entering a shop or bar, greet with “¡Hola!” or “Buenos días”.
  • Use “por favor” (please) and “gracias” (thank you) often—politeness is valued.
  • In Spain, people may greet with a handshake or two kisses (friends/family).
  • “Tú” (informal you) is used with friends/peers; “usted” (formal you) for elders or officials.
  • Shops often close for “siesta” (lunch break) between 2–5pm, especially in small towns.
  • Don’t be afraid to ask for help—locals are usually friendly to pilgrims.
