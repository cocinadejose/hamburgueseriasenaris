Camino Tips: Pilgrim Wisdom

At our hamburger restaurant, every day we see pilgrims passing by who stop to regain strength before continuing their adventure to Santiago. Between sandwiches and burgers, they tell us their stories, their discoveries, and above all, what they have learned on the Camino.

This is our way of giving back to that exchange: compiling these tips and opening this window to the world with the hope that those looking to make the Camino will find something here that may be useful to them. These tips come from both our own experience and the knowledge of pilgrims who take the time to share their experiences with us. Good Camino!
