Curiosities and Stories of the Camino

The Camino de Santiago is much more than a pilgrimage route: it's a tapestry woven with thousands of personal stories, ancient legends and magical moments shared among pilgrims. In our hamburger restaurant, we've heard hundreds of these experiences, and here we want to share the most inspiring ones with you.

This section is a space to reflect on why and how to walk the Camino, based on the experiences that pilgrims tell us when they arrive here. Discover the reasons that move people to undertake this adventure, the anecdotes that arise along the way, and the deep meaning it has for each traveler. ¡Buen Camino!
