The Route: Your Way, Day by Day

Here is the complete breakdown of your adventure from Sarria. We have divided the journey into 6 key days: a crucial preparation day and five days of walking. Click on each stage to see the detailed guide with maps, tips, and secrets.

Stage 0: The Final Preparation

The key day in Santiago or Sarria that will define your Camino. Where to get the credential, what to check, and how to avoid starting like a zombie.

View Stage 0

Stage 1: Sarria → Portomarín

The pilgrim's baptism. 22km through rural Galicia, magical forests, and the famous 100km marker. It all starts here!

View Stage 1

Stage 2: Portomarín → Palas de Rei

A day of steady but gentle ascent that will test you. You will cross the Sierra de Ligonde and enjoy spectacular views.

View Stage 2

Stage 3: Palas de Rei → Arzúa

The longest stage and a mental challenge. You will pass through Melide, famous for its octopus, and enter the heart of Galicia.

View Stage 3

Stage 4: Arzúa → O Pedrouzo

A break for the legs. A shorter, pleasant stage through eucalyptus forests, perfect for recovering your strength before the grand finale.

View Stage 4

Stage 5: O Pedrouzo → Santiago

Emotions running high. The arrival at Monte do Gozo, the first sight of the Cathedral's towers, and the triumphant entry into Plaza del Obradoiro.

View Stage 5
