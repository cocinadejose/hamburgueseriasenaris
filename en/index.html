About Us

We are a family that has devoted its life to serving the best homemade food in Santiago de Compostela. Our commitment is to bring you fresh ingredients, authentic flavours and service that makes you feel at home. Every burger and sandwich is prepared with care using the finest local ingredients.

Our Place

Come and discover our neighbourhood bar in San Pedro, right next to the Seminario Menor. A cosy, traditional spot where you can enjoy our specialities in a laid-back, family atmosphere.

  • 🪑 Comfortable tables, a roomy bar and a functional kitchen where we prepare every dish on the spot.
  • ⛪ Right next to the Seminario Menor and opposite Belvís Park.
  • 🚶 10 minutes from the Food Market and 15 minutes from the Cathedral.
  • 📍 Av. Quiroga Palacios 5, 15703 Santiago de Compostela.
