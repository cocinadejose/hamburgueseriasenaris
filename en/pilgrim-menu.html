🥾 Pilgrim's Menu · €12

Traditional Pilgrim's Menu in Santiago de Compostela

A menu for pilgrims looking for real Galician food, prepared here with the best of our local produce. Homemade Spanish omelette, Galician chorizo from a local butcher, Arzúa-Ulloa PDO cheese, and artisanal Barrantes red wine from O Salnés. Local ingredients, no shortcuts. Everything included for just €12.

We are at Av. Quiroga Palacios 5, next to the Seminario Menor and opposite Belvis Park, on the route of many stages of both the French Way and the Portuguese Way.

Fixed-price menu, served at your table. Seasonal Galician produce, with time to sit down and enjoy it.

Everything we serve is

Made with fresh ingredients from local suppliers · No preservatives or additives · Bread from a neighborhood bakery · Traditional home-style recipes

Our philosophy: traditional food for those on the Way

We have been cooking in Santiago for more than 25 years. We know that after walking 20 to 25 kilometers, your body needs real food, clean energy and ingredients that sit well. That is why we work with local produce and suppliers from here, with no chains and no middlemen.

This menu is very fairly priced because it is not just business for us: it is our way of welcoming you to Santiago and saying congratulations. After so many kilometers, you have earned it. That is why it is reserved exclusively for pilgrims: you can only order it if you are walking the Camino and present your pilgrim credential. It is our small tribute to everyone who arrives on foot.

Served exclusively in our bar

This menu is served only in our bar. Sit down, rest, and enjoy your meal properly after a long stage: at the table, with time, and in a relaxed atmosphere. We do not offer takeaway or delivery for this menu.

For groups of more than 8 people, coaches, agencies or excursions, we also prepare dedicated fixed-price sandwich menus from €5.50, with their own conditions and lead times.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Pilgrim's Menu cost at Señarís?

It is exactly €12, everything included, VAT included, with no surprises.

Do I have to be a pilgrim to order it?

Yes. It is reserved exclusively for pilgrims. You need to be walking the Camino de Santiago and present your pilgrim credential when ordering.

Do I need to book in advance?

No booking is needed. Just come during the menu service hours, from 12:30 PM to 4:00 PM, and bring your credential.

Are there vegetarian or gluten-free options?

Yes. Let us know when you arrive or call us in advance and we will adapt it with no problem and no extra charge.

Are you close to the Seminario Menor hostels?

We are right opposite the main entrance of Seminario Menor. Cross the street and you are here, just a one-minute walk away.
