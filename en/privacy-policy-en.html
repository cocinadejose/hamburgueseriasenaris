🏪 Our Local Philosophy: Why We Don't Offer Online Ordering

Hamburguesería Señarís is a family-owned local business that has been serving the Santiago de Compostela community for over 25 years. Our philosophy is based on personal service and quality direct customer care.

Why don't we offer email orders or an online shopping cart?

  • 🗣️ Direct communication: We prefer our customers to speak with us directly, either by phone or in person. This allows us to clarify doubts, suggest alternatives, and ensure each order is exactly as you want it.
  • 🌍 Language limitations: Although this website is translated into several languages to help international visitors learn about us, our team has limited knowledge of other languages. Managing online orders in multiple languages could lead to errors and misunderstandings.
  • 🎯 Simplicity and authenticity: We don't want to become an impersonal chain. We value human contact and the experience of coming to our establishment, where we can serve you with the warmth and attention you deserve.
  • 📱 Appropriate technology: This website is an informational tool: you can see our menu, hours, location, and decide whether to visit us or call. It's not meant to replace the experience of visiting us in person.

How to place your order?

  • 🚶 Visit us: Av. de Quiroga Palacios, 5, 15703 Santiago de Compostela. Come in person and enjoy our atmosphere.
  • 💬 WhatsApp: If you prefer to write, you can contact us via WhatsApp (preferably in Spanish or Galician).

Thank you for understanding our way of working. We believe the best burger is made with dedication and enjoyed in good company. We look forward to seeing you!
