🚌 Coach Groups · Travel Agencies · Companies

Perfect for short visits to the Cathedral and the city of Santiago

We regularly work with travel agencies, tour operators, coach companies, and schools. We coordinate exact arrival times, drop‑off points, and order quantities. All menus are fully prepared and packaged for the agreed time — no waiting, no queues.

We provide formal contracts, official invoices, and stable rates throughout the year. If you represent an agency or company, call us to arrange custom terms.

🚚 Free delivery anywhere in Santiago

For orders of 10 guests or more

We bring your order directly to your chosen location: official coach stops, squares, hostels, the Cathedral area, museums, parking lots, or your accommodation entrance. All food is kept properly chilled, well‑protected, and delivered on time.

When we arrive near your location, we will call the contact number you provided to arrange a smooth hand‑over. Payment is made at that moment: cash or Bizum.

How to place your order

Just three simple steps — no travel required, no paperwork, no advance payment.

1

Call us

Dial +34 881 08 25 71. Tell us the number of guests, the date, the desired time, and provide your full name and a contact phone number.

2

Choose your meal

Each guest selects their preferred sandwich or burger from our menu. We take note of your choices and confirm the order.

3

Collect or receive

Arrive at our premises at the agreed time and find your order ready. For groups of 10 or more, we deliver it free of charge to any location in the city.

Are you a pilgrim travelling alone or in a small group?

If you are walking the Camino de Santiago, we also offer our Traditional Pilgrim's Menu for €12: homemade Spanish omelette, chorizo with turnip greens, Arzúa‑Ulloa cheese, and local Barrantes wine. Available only to pilgrims with a valid Pilgrim's Credential, served at the table.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay for my order?

Payment is made upon receipt, never in advance. At our premises: cash, card or Bizum. Public delivery points: cash or Bizum only (we do not carry portable card readers). Travel agencies and companies: invoicing and custom terms available.

What is the most popular choice for groups?

By far, our traditional Spanish omelette sandwich — it is our most requested item by groups, coaches, and agencies.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan, or gluten‑free options?

Yes, we have a wide variety available. Just mention your requirements when you call, and we will adapt the order at no extra cost.

Do you work officially with travel agencies?

Absolutely. We have established terms, formal contracts, and invoicing options tailored for businesses, schools, and tour operators.

Where do you deliver?

For orders of 10 guests or more, we deliver free of charge to any point in Santiago de Compostela: coach stops, the Cathedral, public squares, hostels, museums, and parking areas.
